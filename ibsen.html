A Doll's House

Act 1, Scene 1

[SCENE.—A room furnished comfortably and tastefully, but not extravagantly. A bell rings in the hall; shortly afterwards the door is heard to open. Enter NORA, humming a tune and in high spirits.]

NORA

Hide the Christmas Tree carefully, Helen. Be sure the children do not see it until this evening, when it is dressed.

[To the PORTER, taking out her purse.]

How much?

PORTER

Sixpence.

NORA

There is a shilling. No, keep the change.

[The PORTER thanks her, and goes out. NORA shuts the door. She is laughing to herself, as she takes off her hat and coat.]

Yes, he is in.

HELMER

[calls out from his room]

Is that my little lark twittering out there?

NORA

[busy opening some of the parcels]

Yes, it is!

HELMER

Is it my little squirrel bustling about?

NORA

Yes!
